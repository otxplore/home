01 / INTRODUCTION

Overview

OTXS — OTX Services | Discover and Explore — provides online information and digital resources related to travel, exploration, stays, adventures, ride companionship and associated tools.

In this policy, “OTXS”, “we”, “our” and “us” refer to the OTXS service and its operator. “You” refers to a visitor, customer or person using our websites or services.

By using our websites, you agree to use them lawfully and responsibly. If you do not agree with a relevant policy, please discontinue use of the affected service.

02 / YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

We aim to collect only the information reasonably needed to respond to enquiries, coordinate requested services, improve our websites and maintain service records.

Information you may provide

  • Name and contact details, such as email address or phone number.
  • Travel enquiries, requested dates, destinations and preferences.
  • Booking details or information you voluntarily include in a message.
  • Feedback and communications you send to OTXS.

How information may be used

  • To reply to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • To coordinate, confirm or manage a booking where applicable.
  • To maintain records, prevent misuse and address support requests.
  • To improve website functionality and understand service needs.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell personal information. Where necessary to fulfil a request, information may be shared with relevant service providers or partners, or disclosed when legally required. We aim to limit such sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

Information may be retained for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational requirements or applicable legal obligations, and then deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, government identity documents or other sensitive information through ordinary email or public forms unless a secure, appropriate process has been provided.

OTXS websites may use essential browser storage, cookies or analytics tools to support functionality, security and performance, depending on the features deployed on each website.

Third-party embedded content or tools may use their own cookies or collect technical information under their respective policies. Browser settings may allow you to restrict cookies, although some website features may not work as intended.

This section should be updated to identify the actual analytics, hosting, form, advertising and tracking tools used by OTXS. Do not assume that a particular tool is active unless it has been installed and configured.

Website usage

You may browse and use OTXS websites for lawful personal or business purposes, subject to the conditions of each service.

You must not:

  • Submit false enquiries or impersonate another person.
  • Scrape, copy or republish protected material in violation of applicable rights.

OTXS names, branding, original content and website materials may be protected by applicable intellectual-property laws. Permission may be required before reproducing or commercially reusing them.

05 / REQUESTING A SERVICE

Enquiries & bookings

Sending an enquiry, filling out a form or contacting OTXS does not by itself guarantee availability or create a confirmed booking. A booking should be treated as confirmed only after the relevant details and any applicable conditions have been explicitly agreed.

Depending on the service, confirmation may include the date, time, pickup or meeting point, destination, number of participants, price and any special requirements.

Customers should check the details in their confirmation and promptly communicate any errors or changes. OTXS may request additional information reasonably necessary to coordinate a service.

Where OTXS connects users with independent service providers, the provider may have separate terms and responsibilities. The specific arrangement should be made clear before the transaction.

06 / PRICES & TRANSACTIONS

Payments & pricing

Prices, deposits, payment methods and due dates may vary by service. Any applicable charges should be communicated before a booking or paid service is confirmed.

  • Confirm the total price and what is included before paying.
  • Check whether taxes, entry fees, meals, fuel or other expenses are included.
  • Use only payment methods communicated through an authorised OTXS channel.
  • Keep payment confirmations and receipts for your records.

Do not assume that an online enquiry, estimate, displayed price or informal discussion is a final quote. Any price change should be communicated and agreed upon before proceeding.

OTXS will not ask you to disclose your banking password, card PIN or one-time password to process an ordinary payment.

07 / CHANGES & REFUNDS

Cancellations, rescheduling & refunds

Cancellation and refund conditions depend on the service and the costs or commitments involved. Where a specific policy applies, it should be communicated before payment or booking confirmation.

Customer cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact OTXS as early as possible. Any refund, deduction or rescheduling fee will depend on the terms disclosed for that booking and applicable law.

Provider cancellations or changes

If OTXS or a relevant service provider cannot deliver a confirmed service, we will communicate the available next steps. Depending on the circumstances and agreed terms, these may include rescheduling, an alternative arrangement or a refund of eligible amounts paid.

Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, the processing time may depend on the payment method and financial service provider. Any applicable refund timeline should be communicated to the customer.

This general section does not establish a universal non-refundable deposit or fixed cancellation deadline. Service-specific conditions should be documented before accepting payment.

08 / RESPONSIBLE EXPLORATION

Travel, adventure & ride safety

Travel and outdoor activities may involve road, weather, terrain, health and other risks. Visitors and participants should make informed decisions and follow applicable laws and local guidance.

  • Follow traffic rules, local restrictions and instructions from authorised personnel.
  • Use appropriate safety equipment and protective clothing.
  • Check weather, road conditions, route suitability and vehicle readiness before departure.
  • Share accurate information about your requirements and relevant travel constraints.
  • Do not participate in an activity if you believe conditions are unsafe.
  • Respect local communities, private property, wildlife and the natural environment.

Motorcycle rides

If a service involves travelling by motorcycle, participants must be comfortable with the arrangement and use legally required protective equipment. The rider and passenger must comply with applicable road-safety laws. Motorcycle travel may be less protective than travel in an enclosed vehicle.

A destination guide, route suggestion or estimated travel time is informational and does not guarantee road conditions, safety or arrival time. Conditions can change without notice.

Personal responsibility

Participants should assess their own comfort, fitness, equipment and ability to take part. Contact emergency services directly if there is an immediate threat to life or safety; do not rely solely on an online message or website response.
